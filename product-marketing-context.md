# Product Marketing Context — Sehle (سهلة)

This master document defines the positioning, messaging, target personas, and brand voice for Sehle (سهلة), a leading custom software solutions provider in Iraq and the Middle East.

---

## 1. Core Positioning & Value Proposition

**Elevator Pitch:**
Sehle builds custom software, enterprise ERPs, and digital transformation solutions that solve complex business problems for Iraqi and Middle Eastern enterprises. We pair European engineering standards and modern cloud architectures with deep local market understanding, offering fully bilingual (Arabic/English) systems that work offline or online.

**Key Value Propositions:**
1. **Designed for the Iraqi Market:** Built specifically around Middle Eastern business workflows, local tax regulations, bilingual requirements, and unreliable network environments (supporting offline-first state sync).
2. **Enterprise-Grade Engineering:** We build bespoke systems (ERP, CRM, inventory, HR) that replace fragmented Excel sheets and outdated legacy software with fast, secure cloud and mobile apps.
3. **End-to-End Partnership:** We don't just write code; we conduct in-depth operational audits, design custom architectures, manage migration from legacy databases, and provide ongoing local support.

---

## 2. Target Personas

### Persona A: The Iraqi SME Owner & Founder
- **Profile:** Owns a growing trading, logistics, or retail business in Baghdad or Erbil.
- **Pain Points:** 
  - Fragmented operations (sales, stock, accounting in separate silos).
  - Lack of real-time visibility into inventory across multiple warehouses.
  - Off-the-shelf software (e.g. QuickBooks) is not localized or culturally intuitive for their staff.
- **Objectives:** Automate manual reporting, reduce inventory shrinkage, and scale operations.
- **Differentiator Pitch:** "An all-in-one custom management system that fits your business like a glove, speaking both Arabic and English, accessible on mobile."

### Persona B: The Corporate Enterprise Executive / CIO
- **Profile:** Tech lead or decision-maker at a large Iraqi enterprise (oil & gas, telecom, private banking, large construction).
- **Pain Points:**
  - High cybersecurity risks and data leaks from unvalidated legacy systems.
  - Outdated technology stacks that cannot scale or integrate with modern APIs.
  - Dependence on foreign offshore agencies that lack local support.
- **Objectives:** Modernize legacy workflows, enforce high data security/compliance, and achieve digital transformation.
- **Differentiator Pitch:** "High-performance, secure software built by senior engineers with on-the-ground support in Iraq and Turkey."

---

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone

- **Authoritative & Professional:** Speak as industry leaders in technology and software architecture.
- **Empathetic & Solution-Oriented:** Focus on the customer's operational pain points first, then show how technology solves them.
- **Direct & Transparent:** Avoid empty tech jargon. Explain complex topics (ERP, database synchronization) in simple, outcome-driven business terms.
- **Localized & Culturally Resonant:** Use professional Modern Standard Arabic that feels natural to Iraqi business professionals.
